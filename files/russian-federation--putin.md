### 2026-07-03 — Joint Force auxiliary command post

Kremlin transcript records Putin chairing a meeting with General Staff leaders and group commanders.

- Putin: "The liberation of the Lugansk People's Republic has recently been completed in full."
- Putin stated that operations continued in Donetsk, Zaporozhye, and Kherson, and that a border security zone was being established in parts of Kharkov, Sumy, and Dnepropetrovsk.
- Putin stated that Russian forces had taken 133 settlements and more than 3,000 square kilometres since the start of 2026.
- Putin ordered review of summer-period tasks and support for ongoing offensive operations.
- After Gerasimov's report, Putin directed continued analysis of the states identified by Gerasimov as "instigators" and said the analysis would be used for later decisions.
